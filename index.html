Courthouse Digital Signage

Frank E. Moss U.S. Courthouse — District of Utah

A real-time digital signage system for displaying court hearing calendars, announcements, and emergency notifications on HDMI-connected displays throughout the courthouse. Hearing data is automatically imported by scraping PDF calendars published by the U.S. Bankruptcy Court.

Automated Calendar Import

PDFs from utb.uscourts.gov are scraped, parsed, and pushed to displays in real time.

  • Six judges supported
  • Configurable import schedule
  • Stale entry auto-cleanup

Display Management

Each display is independently configured with its own filters, layout, and theme.

  • Courtroom, lobby, wayfinding, and IT status templates
  • Portrait and landscape orientation
  • Live preview from the admin portal

Emergency Overrides

Three severity levels for instant emergency notifications across all displays.

  • Section, content area, or full screen takeover
  • WebSocket push in under 1 second
  • Activation audit logging
